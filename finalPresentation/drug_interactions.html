The gap: The FDA collects millions of adverse event reports per year, but most are analyzed manually. No automated system detects novel DDI signals at scale.
Our approach: Mine real FAERS reports to (A) discover which drug combinations are statistically dangerous, and (B) predict whether a new patient case will have a severe outcome.
What is FAERS?
FDA Adverse Event Reporting System — the world's largest pharmacovigilance database. Each report contains: patient demographics, drugs taken, reactions, and outcome severity.
Real-world data. Real patients. Real interactions.
The FDA has it — we mine it.

TASK A: MARKET BASKET MODEL + 3 KEY METRICS

Market Basket Model
Each FAERS report = 1 transaction. Items = drugs + reactions.

{METHOTREXATE, PREDNISONE, NAUSEA, FATIGUE}

We compared two encodings:
  • Active substances (generic names) — consolidates brand fragmentation
  • Brand names — more noise, lower quality rules
Winner: Active substances — same rules, less noise.
Indication Bias Filter (Step 6A)
Raw rules contain false positives like:
METHOTREXATE → RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS
That's why the drug is prescribed, not an adverse effect.
Filter removes all "indication" consequents → 13 genuine DDI signals.
3 Key Metrics — what they mean

Support — how often the combo appears in reports
≥ 0.25% of 108k reports = threshold

Confidence — P(reaction | drug combo)
avg 0.637 across our 13 rules

Lift — how much MORE than random chance
Lift = 223 → 223× above baseline rate. This is the key metric.

✗ Leakage Detected & Removed

rxn_hospitalisation appeared as the #2 most important feature in early XGBoost (10.7% gain).

On inspection: this MedDRA term directly encodes seriousnesshospitalization=1 — which IS the target variable.

Confirmation test: Removed it → AUC dropped 0.805 → 0.796. The drop confirmed the leakage was real.
12 death/hospitalization MedDRA terms excluded.
AUC 0.796 = clean baseline.
AUC 0.805 (final) = Optuna tuning on top of clean data.

Why replace SVM?
SVM collapsed to all-negative predictions on this imbalanced dataset — AUC ≈ 0.63 even with class_weight='balanced'. XGBoost/CatBoost handle imbalance natively and train in <3s on GPU.
Threshold tuning: Grid search [0.10–0.70] on val set, maximizing F1-severe. Optimal ≈ 0.51–0.54 (not default 0.5).

TASK B: EVALUATION — CONFUSION MATRICES & PR CURVES

Precision-Recall curves — more informative than accuracy for imbalanced data

Why AUC, not accuracy?
Always-predict-"not severe" = 73.4% accuracy. AUC measures ranking ability regardless of imbalance.
Tuned Ensemble: Recall-severe = 0.651
False Negatives (missed severe cases) are clinically more dangerous than False Positives.
Precision-severe ≈ 0.53
Acceptable for a screening tool. Higher precision would be needed for clinical deployment.

FUTURE WORK & REFERENCES
